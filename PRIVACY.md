# Privacy Policy for Quran 786 TV

**Effective date:** [Date]

Quran 786 TV ("the app") is a free, ad-free application for playing Quran verses and translations.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information about users. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking tools, cookies, or user accounts.

## Internet Usage

The app requests Internet access solely for the following purposes:

- Streaming or downloading Quran audio files.
- Fetching translation content in multiple languages.

No personal data, device identifiers, or user information is sent by the app.

## Third-Party Services

The app may connect to third-party servers to retrieve audio and translation content. These services may receive standard technical request information (such as IP address) as part of normal Internet communication. We do not control these third-party services, and their own privacy policies may apply.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
